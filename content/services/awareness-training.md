Generic annual e-learning rarely changes behaviour. Our training is short, concrete, and
mapped to what each role actually faces — a finance team learns invoice and approvals
fraud; developers work through a real vulnerable code example; executives see how a
targeted attack against them would be built. Every session ends with three things the
attendee will do differently tomorrow.

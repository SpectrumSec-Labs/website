Knowing what is misconfigured is not the same as fixing it. This engagement is
deliberately hands-on: we review your cloud, identity, endpoint, and network
configuration, agree a prioritised backlog, then work alongside your team to apply and
test each change — preferably as code, so the improvement is permanent and reviewable.

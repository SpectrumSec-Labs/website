Red teaming starts from an objective, not a target list. We emulate the tradecraft of a
relevant threat actor to reach that objective, staying deliberately quiet to test your
detection and response. The value is in the debrief: a shared timeline of what happened
versus what your SOC saw, and a prioritised set of detections to close the gaps.

Best run once you have already addressed the findings from a penetration test — red
teaming is about resilience, not first-pass vulnerability discovery.

Phishing simulation is only useful if it is realistic, ethical, and measured over time.
We design scenarios around what your people would plausibly receive, run them with
safe infrastructure that never stores a real password, and focus the outcome on
**reporting rate** — because a workforce that reports quickly is worth more than one
that simply clicks less.

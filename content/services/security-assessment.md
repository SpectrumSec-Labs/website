Where a penetration test asks *"can this be broken?"*, an assessment asks *"is our
security programme fit for the risks we carry?"* We combine interviews, documentation
review, and hands-on configuration sampling to build an evidence-based picture of your
posture — then translate it into a plan with owners, effort, and sequence.

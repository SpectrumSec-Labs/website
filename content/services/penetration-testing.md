A penetration test answers a specific question: *if a motivated attacker targeted this
system, what could they actually do?* We go beyond scanner output — manually verifying
findings, chaining lower-severity issues into meaningful compromise, and documenting each
step so your team can reproduce and fix it.

## How it works

1. **Scoping call.** We agree targets, rules of engagement, testing windows, and
   emergency contacts. You get a fixed price.
2. **Testing.** Daily progress notes; immediate escalation for anything critical.
3. **Reporting.** An executive summary, a prioritised findings list with evidence, and
   concrete remediation guidance.
4. **Retest.** Once you have remediated, we re-test the affected areas at no extra cost.

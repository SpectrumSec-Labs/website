We take the security of our own systems seriously. If you believe you have found a
vulnerability in this website, our infrastructure, or something we have published, we
want to hear from you.

## How to report

- Email `security@spectrumsec.eu`, encrypted to our [PGP key](/pgp-key.txt) where
  possible.
- Include enough detail to reproduce the issue: URLs, payloads, and the impact you
  observed.
- Please give us a reasonable time to remediate before any public disclosure.

## Scope

In scope: `spectrumsec.eu` and its subdomains, and content we author.

Out of scope: findings that require physical access, social engineering of our staff,
denial-of-service testing, automated scanner output without a demonstrated impact, and
third-party services we link to.

## Our commitment

- We will acknowledge your report within **3 working days**.
- We will keep you updated on remediation progress.
- We will credit you (if you wish) once the issue is resolved.
- We will not pursue legal action for good-faith research that respects this policy.

## Not a bug bounty

We do not currently operate a paid bounty programme, but we are grateful for every
good-faith report and will acknowledge contributions publicly.

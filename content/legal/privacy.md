## Who we are

SpectrumSec ("we") is the data controller for personal data processed through this
website and in the course of our services. Contact:
[hello@spectrumsec.eu](mailto:hello@spectrumsec.eu).

## What we collect

- **Website:** this site sets no cookies. Our self-hosted analytics
  ([Umami](/legal/privacy/#analytics)) records aggregate, non-identifying usage data and
  does not store personal data or use cross-site identifiers. Server access logs
  (including IP address) are retained for a short period for security and
  troubleshooting.
- **Enquiries:** if you email us, we process the contents of your message and your
  contact details to respond and, where relevant, to deliver services.

## Legal bases

Legitimate interests (responding to enquiries, securing our infrastructure) and, where
applicable, performance of a contract.

## Analytics {#analytics}

We use a self-hosted instance of Umami. No data is shared with third parties. The
analytics endpoint is served from our own domain.

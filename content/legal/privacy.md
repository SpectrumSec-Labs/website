## Who we are

SpectrumSec ("we") is the data controller for personal data processed through this
website and in the course of our services. Contact:
[contact@spectrumsec.eu](mailto:contact@spectrumsec.eu).

## What we collect

**Visiting this website.** This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from third
parties. We keep request logs (such as IP address, date and time, page requested and
browser type) for security and to produce visit statistics. Statistics are aggregate
or at organisation level. We do not identify individual visitors or build profiles.

**Contacting us.** If you email us, we process your message and contact details to
reply and, where relevant, to deliver our services.

## How long we keep it

Request logs are kept for 3 months. After that, IP addresses are shortened so they
no longer identify a connection. Enquiries are kept as long as needed to handle them
and to meet our legal obligations.

## Who has access

Only SpectrumSec. Your data is processed on our own systems, hosted in the EU. We do
not sell or share it, and no third party analyses it on our behalf.

## Legal bases

- Legitimate interests: keeping the website secure, visit statistics, replying to
  enquiries.
- Contract: where you are or are becoming a client.

## Your rights

You can access, correct or erase your data, restrict or object to its processing,
and complain to your data-protection authority. Email
[contact@spectrumsec.eu](mailto:contact@spectrumsec.eu).

## Service providers

We use a small number of providers that process data on our behalf, under
data-processing agreements:

- Hosting provider (servers in the EU)
- Email provider (data stored in the EU)

Both belong to groups based outside the EU. Any access from outside the EU is
covered by EU-approved safeguards (standard contractual clauses and the EU–US
Data Privacy Framework).
